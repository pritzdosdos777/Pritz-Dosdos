One of the questions we hear all the time is, what exactly is spinal decompression? The easiest way to understand it is to think about what happens to your spine over time. Between the bones of your spine are discs that act like cushions. Over the years, because of aging, injuries, repetitive work, or just everyday wear and tear, those discs and joints can become compressed and irritated. That can contribute to problems like back pain, disc bulges or herniations, and sometimes pressure or irritation around the nerves, which may cause pain, numbness, tingling, or sciatica. Spinal decompression is a non-surgical treatment designed to gently stretch and mobilize the spine and reduce some of that pressure. Here at Anderson Chiropractic, we use a system called Back-on-Trac. Instead of lying down and being strapped into a traditional traction table, you're comfortably seated while the system gently stretches and mobilizes different areas of your spine. Most patients actually find it very comfortable and relaxing. Now, spinal decompression isn't a miracle cure, and it isn't right for everyone. That's why we first evaluate what's actually causing your problem. If you're dealing with chronic back pain, sciatica, or disc-related problems, and you'd like to know whether decompression might be an option, give us a call. We'd be happy to take a look.
